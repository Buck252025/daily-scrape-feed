## Top Story
Apple's Siri is now modular—code shows you can swap the default AI for Claude or ChatGPT. This signals a massive shift toward open-stack AI integration in consumer products, meaning consultants selling AI services have a new wedge: help SMBs and enterprises customize their existing tools with best-in-class models instead of rip-and-replace migrations.

## What People Are Saying
- Boris Cherny (Claude Code lead) is coaching developers on how to actually *use* Claude productively—don't just run it blind. His Threads post frames the real work: set quality bars, use the latest frontier models (Opus 5 or Fable 5.1), and invest in teaching Claude your codebase via a CLAUDE.md file. This flips the narrative from "AI replaces engineers" to "engineers who know how to prompt win."
- The self-hosted LLM migration wave is real. Patrick McCanna documented gotchas moving 35kb preprompts from Anthropic/OpenAI to Ollama, and it hit 75 points on HN with substantive comments—people are actively cost-optimizing and reducing vendor lock-in by moving to open models.
- "AI doom is hype" is now a mainstream talking point on HN (104 points, 85 comments on Erkan Saka's post), with builders pushing back against doomsaying as performative. This creates room for consultants to position themselves as pragmatists selling outcomes, not fear.
