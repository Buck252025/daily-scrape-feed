## What People Are Saying
- Engineers are obsessing over "cognitive debt"—the idea that blindly accepting LLM-generated code without understanding it creates technical and organizational risk (182 points, 138 comments). The counterintuitive take: manually retyping AI output forces comprehension and prevents downstream failures.
- Boris Cherny (Claude Code lead) posted on Threads that layering defenses—model alignment + prompt injection probes + Auto Mode—reduces prompt injection attack success to ~0%, suggesting the security conversation is moving from "is AI safe?" to "how do we architect safety into production systems?"
- DeepSeek's V4 Flash model iteration (via @gigaqian on TikTok) and AirLLM's 70B inference on a single 4GB GPU (50 points) indicate the efficiency frontier is moving faster than expected, quietly undermining the "you need enterprise GPU clusters" narrative that many vendors depend on.
