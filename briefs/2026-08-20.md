## Top Story
Claude's agentic capabilities are moving from proof-of-concept to production workflows. Boris Cherny (Claude Code lead) posted on Threads that his team has automated routine code changes at scale—388 PRs opened in weeks, 180 merged after human review—with Claude getting most right on the first shot. This signals that AI agents aren't just hype; they're now reliably handling mechanical, repetitive work that teams can iterate on and improve daily. For consultants and SMB owners, this is the moment to stop asking "can AI agents work?" and start asking "what processes can we hand off?"
