## Top Story
OpenAI just published 700 mathematical proofs and counterexamples, signaling a major shift in how AI labs are now competing on *capability transparency* rather than closed benchmarks. For consultants selling AI services, this matters because clients are moving from "trust us, we're the best" to "here's the proof"—and your sales pitch needs to evolve from claims to demonstrated artifacts. Hacker News engagement (1,160 points on the math progress post) shows the technical community is watching this closely.

## What People Are Saying
- Boris Cherny (Claude Code lead) posted on Threads that modern prompting has fundamentally shifted: "Don't overly scaffold, don't be prescriptive for most tasks — give Claude a goal, and it will figure it out." This directly contradicts 2024-era prompting best practices, suggesting that newer models (Claude Sonnet 3.5+) require a *conversational, minimal-instruction approach* rather than detailed system prompts. For SMBs adopting Claude, this is a licensing-and-workflow reset.
- GaryVee's recurring theme across multiple uploads ("your marketing is stuck in 1992," "Live Shopping Is the Biggest Opportunity You're Ignoring") suggests he's doubling down on *real-time, synchronous commerce* as the next frontier—a signal that static AI chatbots and async automation may already feel dated to the fastest-moving operators.
- A Bloomberg study showing Claude and ChatGPT offer different shopping prices based on perceived wealth (65 points on HN) has triggered conversation about *AI bias in commerce*—consultants need to know this is becoming a compliance and reputation risk, not just an ethics footnote.

## Updates
