"Astra for Law" (548 HN points, 645 comments) signals OpenAI is shipping vertical-specific AI products, but the conversation is being drowned out by security concerns—a heap overflow and SSO misconfiguration in OpenAI's own internal repos (405 points) and Microsoft/OpenAI losing a court fight to hide docs admitting AI scraping is "the largest theft of labor in human history." ## What People Are Saying
- **LLM application is shifting from "what can we build" to "how do we actually use this without breaking things."** "How to Write with an LLM" (251 points, 171 comments) and "LLM Classification Is Feature Engineering" (109 points) suggest the discourse has matured past hype—practitioners are now debating whether LLMs are actually the right tool for the job, not whether to use them at all.
- **Enterprise AI agents are consolidating around portability and composability.** Skillsync (YC W26) launching as "AI chat sessions made portable across agents" (60 points, 53 comments) reveals a real gap: businesses don't want vendor lock-in. This is a selling point for consultants positioning AI integration as infrastructure-agnostic.
- **Privacy and data exfiltration are becoming deal-killers.** ZCode silently uploading Git history (222 points) and the broader OpenAI/Microsoft scraping lawsuit trending hard signals that SMB decision-makers are now asking "what data leaves my company?" before adopting tools—consultants need to front-load this conversation.
