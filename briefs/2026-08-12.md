## Top Story
Anthropic's multi-layered defense stack—combining strong model alignment, prompt injection probes, and Auto Mode in Claude Code—is now achieving ~0% success rates on prompt injection attacks. Boris Cherny (Claude Code lead) posted on Threads that this represents a meaningful shift in LLM safety posture, moving from theoretical mitigation to demonstrated resilience in production. For consultants and integrators selling Claude-based automation, this is credibility ammunition: you can now pitch "injection-resistant" as a solved problem, not a risk.
