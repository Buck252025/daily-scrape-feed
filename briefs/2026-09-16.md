Combined with the PS5 Linux lead's viral Hacker News post (175 points) blaming open-source decay on "a bunch of noobs using LLMs" without understanding them, there's a clear tension emerging: AI tooling is democratizing faster than expertise can scale, and the people building at the frontier are visibly frustrated.
