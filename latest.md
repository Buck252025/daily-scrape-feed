## Top Story
LLM pricing has collapsed 80% in months (OpenAI and Anthropic leading the race), and this is forcing a fundamental shift in how consultants and SMB owners should think about AI ROI. The commoditization story is real—it's no longer about *if* you can afford AI tools, it's about *which problems are worth solving with them*. This changes the sales narrative entirely: your pitch moves from "cost savings through automation" to "competitive advantage through speed and differentiation."
